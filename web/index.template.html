Privacy & account information

Messages, selected saved context, and image/project prompts are sent to the configured AI provider. Do not send passwords or confidential information. No server-side chat history is stored by this application; your hosting and provider may retain operational data under their own policies.

Memory is saved on this browser, per account. Delete it using Memory bank → Delete saved context. Clearing chat removes visible conversation history but does not delete provider records. For account deletion or provider data requests, contact your deployment administrator.

Email verification and administrator approval are required. Generated code is downloaded as source files and never executed here. Review it before running. NEXUS is an AI assistant, not ASI; memory stores context and does not train model weights.

DEFENCE SUPPORT WORKSPACE · Offline analysis

Administrative, facilities, training, humanitarian and defensive cyber reporting. All calculations occur locally in your browser; nothing entered in this panel is sent to an AI provider. No classified or tactical information.

This is a decision-support aid, not a military-certified system. Reports cannot command people, control assets, target anyone, or execute operations.
